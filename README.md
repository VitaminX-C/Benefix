## 🌟 Overview

**BENEFIX** is an AI-powered **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** platform that connects **students** and **young entrepreneurs** with government schemes they're actually eligible for.

Instead of forcing users to navigate hundreds of government websites and PDFs, SetuAI intelligently analyzes a user's profile and recommends the most relevant schemes in seconds.

Think of it as an AI bridge between **people** and **opportunities**.

---

## ❌ The Problem

Every year, thousands of scholarships, startup grants, subsidies, and support programs go unused because:

- 📑 Information is scattered across countless government portals
- 🔍 Finding relevant schemes is time-consuming
- 📜 Eligibility criteria are difficult to understand
- 🤷 Many people don't even know these schemes exist

The result?

> **Opportunities remain hidden while deserving people miss out.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Benefix uses a **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** pipeline to simplify discovery.

The system:

1. 👤 Collects relevant user information
2. 📚 Searches a curated knowledge base of government schemes
3. 🧠 Uses AI to understand eligibility and requirements
4. 🎯 Recommends personalized schemes
5. 💬 Explains *why* each scheme matches the user

No endless searching.

No confusing PDFs.

Just relevant opportunities.

# 🚀 Why RAG?

Traditional chatbots generate answers from what they've learned.

SetuAI goes one step further.

It **retrieves** the latest government documents first and then generates responses grounded in those official sources, making recommendations more accurate, reliable, and up-to-date.
